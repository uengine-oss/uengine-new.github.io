# A tour of the screen

> Take one lap around Home cards, Chat, the top bar (Chat · Projects · Folders, Messages, Search, Settings), day and night, and the phone layout. This app has a single screen, and you ask for almost everything through one input box in the middle.

## One screen

You finished your first login and mail connection in Chapter 1, so before the hands-on work, take a lap around the screen. Unlike business systems with many menus and pages, Process GPT Lite works on **a single screen**. On the left, the things to take care of now pile up as cards (Home). In the middle is your conversation with the assistant, and at the bottom is the input box. Whatever you do, you ask for it in words in the input box, and the result comes back as a card in the conversation.

## Home — what to take care of now

Home is a collection of cards the assistant puts up to say "Take care of this now." You connected the demo mailbox in Chapter 1, so the assistant read the five emails and made cards for the four that need action (it skipped one newsletter).

The English word at the start of each card is **what the assistant will do for you**. Click a card and the assistant starts that work in Chat ([Chapter 5](mail.html)).

| Verb | Meaning | Verb | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reply | Reply to it | Review | Review it |
| Pay | Pay it | Call | Call someone |
| Release | Take care of it (release a hold) | Book | Book it |
| Remind | Reminder | Sign | Sign it |
| Add | Add it | | |

When you hover over a card, an × appears at its top right so you can clear it. Home also collects these cards.

| Card | When it appears | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| Decision · Input | When a step of a process is assigned to you. For steps with choices (such as approvals), you decide right on the card | [Chapter 18](process-run.html) |
| Share · Mention · Request | When a teammate sends you a file or calls you with @ | [Chapter 14](sharing.html) |
| Suggestion · Constraint | When a process is suggested from repeated work (administrators), or when a new bottleneck appears in a process | [Chapter 19](mining.html)·[Chapter 20](bottleneck.html) |
| Draft review | When a customer email asks for documents and the assistant has made drafts in advance | [Chapter 9](email-to-quote.html) |

| New panel | The button at the very bottom. Click it and describe the panel you want | [Chapter 12](panel-apps.html) |

## Chat — one long conversation

Chat in the middle is **one long conversation**. You don't need to make new chat rooms. You can ask about a hotel for a business trip in the middle of talking about Moonlight Coffee, and mail briefs and the results of scheduled tasks also pile up here. Mixing topics is fine because the assistant decides, for each message, which story it follows up on, and **keeps it in a separate conversation by topic**. Once there are two or more conversations, **N conversations · view by context** appears at the top of Chat ([Chapter 4](contexts.html)).

Here are the small marks worth noticing in Chat.

- **Date line** — Where the day changes, a date like "Wednesday, October 28" appears.
- **··· Read** — Appears under the last message you sent. It means the assistant received it.
- **Status line** — While the assistant works, what it's doing now appears faintly just above the input box, like "Searching" or "Creating document."
- **Divider** — Work the assistant started on its own, like a "Morning brief" or "Mail · (client) quote request," goes under a divider.

The three tabs **Chat · Projects · Folders** at the top are your workspaces.

| Space | What it's for | Chapter |
|---|---|---|

## Day and night

The sky in the background isn't a picture. It's **the sky at your location right now**. The app calculates the sun's height to pick the colors, and it reflects the weather (clouds, rain) too. When the sun sets, the whole screen switches to the night theme.

![Night — the same screen switches to a dark theme after sunset](img/tour-night.webp)

In **Settings › System**, you can choose the theme from **Auto, Day and Night**. Move the **Sky preview** slider to preview the sky and theme at another time, and click **In real time** to go back. **Check mail › Check now** on the same screen reads your mail right away instead of waiting for the next cycle. If you connected the demo mailbox, **Get practice email** is at the very bottom, where you can pick fictional client emails for the exercises ([Chapter 5](mail.html)).

![System — theme, sky preview, check mail, get practice email](img/tour-settings-system.webp)

!!! tip "Fix the time with the address"
    Add `?hour=21` to the end of the address to open the sky at that time. Use something like `?hour=14` when you always want a presentation screen to show daytime. The screenshots in this tutorial were taken that way too.

## Phones and Android

On a narrow screen (a phone), the layout changes. **Home · Chat · Projects · Folders** tabs appear at the top, Home becomes a full-screen tab, and the input box sits below it. Team messages go into a card at the top of Home.

![Phone width — the Home tab with the input box below it](img/tour-mobile.webp)

On Android, there's also an app you can use by just entering the server address ([demo video](https://youtu.be/TYg7S1WJ0YQ)). To make the install PC reachable from other devices, see "Ports and addresses" in [Chapter 1](install.html).

<details class="answer" markdown="1"><summary>Answer</summary>

1. The weekday, date and time appear in large type.
2. Messages containing `quote` come up with their dates, like the Morning brief sentence "…Northwind's 2027 maintenance quote is due Friday…". Search looks in the messages left in Chat, not in the original emails.
3. 7 PM is after sunset, so with the theme on **Auto** it switches to the night theme. If you fixed it to **Day**, only the sky changes and the theme stays the same.
4. Remind (a request to speak at a seminar — reminds you to reply), Review (a payment on hold because a tax invoice wasn't issued — issue it to release the hold), Pay (a VAT prepayment notice — pay it), Reply (a maintenance quote request — confirm the quote and send it). The verbs and wording differ a little every run. The same email sometimes comes up as Reply instead of Remind, Release instead of Review, or Add instead of Reply.
</details>
