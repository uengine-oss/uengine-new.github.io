Industry Solutions · ERP

uEngine ERP

An ERP redesigned for agentic AI. Because it is event-sourced, you can always recover, even when an agent makes a mistake.

Open the appTutorialDemo videos

Open source (MIT). Your data stays in your own Supabase project, and the app is static files, so there is no server to run. Email sending, e-tax invoices and automatic bank feeds are not included. All video and forecast figures come from a made-up company.

Why build ERP from the ground up?

Agents are starting to post vouchers, make payments, and move inventory in ERP. ERP now needs original records of who did what, which request led to it, and which actions belong together, as well as current numbers. These records help it undo only the mistaken work and answer audit questions.

This task ID is called a saga ID. These are actual execution records from a test with the virtual company Gaon Appliances, where Process GPT Lite processed one tax invoice through MCP and undid it after the request “I'll cancel what we just did”; row names are exactly as recorded in the ledger.

The ledger is the sourceCurrent numbers are calculated from ledger rows. This lets us rebuild the numbers for any point in time.
One task, one IDThe bill, payment, goods receipt, journal entries, and tax totals from one agent request share an ID. The system reads this ID to find the scope of work to undo.
Built in from the startAdding history can help a state-based ERP, but it effectively creates another ledger, and the two can disagree. That is why we rebuilt how ERP stores its records.

Not every system needs this approach, and it costs storage space and short delays between modules, so start with systems where agents change transactions, inventory, and payments.

Read more about why we use event sourcing

An ERP that records every change as an event

Instead of overwriting values, it keeps what happened. So you can see any past moment exactly as it was, undo a mistake after checking what it touches, and use the history to test a change before you make it.

Modules meet only through events. Turning on inventory later changes nothing in the sales module, and when one module stops, the others keep working.

The agent does the work, and one sentence undoes it

Upload just an image of a tax invoice to Process GPT Lite. The agent asks whether to process it in ERP, then uses MCP to post the bill, pay it, receive the goods, record the VAT entry and post the journal entries.

Say "Cancel what you just did" and only the documents from that work are undone. Nothing is deleted: the 21 rows of processing and the 21 rows that cancel them both remain, and the net change in stock and accounts is 0. If the goods have already left the warehouse in the meantime, it does not undo and tells you why.

Start with the basics, add modules as you grow, and keep all your data connected

These screens come from the real app. The data belongs to Gaon Appliances, a made-up company.

Modules

Turn them on one at a time

Only sales is on at first. Any combination works, and each module you turn on adds menus and the forecasts it makes possible.

  • Failure isolationIf inventory stops, orders keep coming in. Once it is fixed, the waiting events are processed with nothing lost.
  • Turning on laterTurn on accounting later and it rebuilds the journal from past invoices and payments. What it cannot know, like stock levels before inventory was on, it tells you.
  • PluginsAdd a module with one SQL file, without changing the existing ones.

Daily work

From quote to payment, from purchase order to bill

Quotes, orders, deliveries, reordering, purchasing, receipts, invoices, payments and journal entries connect through events.

  • InventoryWarehouses, receipts and deliveries, stock adjustments, reorder rules, average cost.
  • Invoicing and accountingInvoices, credit notes, payments, trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, tax summary, period locks.
  • BankImport a statement file and match it against payments. There is no automatic bank feed.
▶ Ch. 1 Start with sales▶ Ch. 3 Purchasing, invoicing, accounting

Time travel

See it as it was, check the scope, then undo

Pick a date and see the orders, stock and receivables of that moment. An undo deletes nothing; it adds one more event.

  • Cascade previewUndoing an order first shows the delivery and stock that would go with it, across modules.
  • Stops what must not be undoneA posted invoice blocks the undo and points you to a credit note. Dates before a lock are blocked too.
  • Reasons and historyWho undid what and why stays in the ledger, and an undo can itself be undone.
▶ Ch. 4 Time travel and rollback

Forecasts and what-ifs

Test it before you change it

Change order quantities, drop a product, move stock between warehouses, or change lead times or payment terms, and see how sales and stockouts shift, with an 80% range. The ledger does not change.

  • No evidence, no numberEach model shows as locked, short of evidence, or ready.
  • It says so when it cannot tellIf the range includes zero, it says first that the effect cannot be distinguished.
  • More modules, more modelsSales alone gives statistics and demand forecasts. Inventory opens reorder tests, and purchasing opens lead-time tests.
▶ Ch. 5 Forecasts and what-ifs

Integration

REST and MCP without building them by hand

Define a type and its REST resource appears. Agents work with the documents of enabled modules through four tools. There is no separate server process.

  • RESTStandard CRUD, version checks, idempotency keys.
  • MCPFour MCP tools—read, create, update, and delete—handle documents; changing a document's state is the business operation itself. Agents use the same tools to undo their own mistakes, but only for their own MCP operations with no dependent work.
  • API keysNever exceed the member's rights and can be revoked at any time. Only a hash is stored.

Product overview

Product overview

Turning on modules, time travel, rollback and what-ifs in one go.

With Process GPT Lite

Tax invoice processing and undo

Upload a file and it asks. Accept and it works over MCP. Say cancel and the saga compensates. When it must not undo, it stops.

Forecasts and what-ifs asked in chat

Ask what sells in which quarter next year and what happens if you order more. The same models as the screens work out the numbers.

Tutorial (Korean)

Ch. 1 Start with sales

Install, create the company, and handle quotes and orders with only sales on.

Ch. 2 Inventory and failure isolation

With inventory on, order events create delivery slips. If inventory stops, sales continues.

Ch. 3 Purchasing, invoicing, accounting

From reordering through receipts, invoices, payments and the trial balance. Accounting rebuilds past entries even when turned on later.

Ch. 4 Time travel and rollback

See a past moment as it was, and undo a mistake across modules after checking the scope.

Ch. 5 Forecasts and what-ifs

Numbers only when there is evidence. Test orders, discontinuation, stock moves, lead times and payment terms before changing them.

Ch. 6 Plugins and integration

Attach a plugin with one SQL file, and connect through generated REST and four MCP tools.

Ch. 7 One voucher through to the VAT return

One purchase tax invoice carries through to stock, the tax invoice record, the books and the VAT return. Closing locks the documents of that period.

Ch. 8 Books, year-end close and fixed assets

Register an asset, run depreciation and work through last year’s close step by step. Check the statements and books against the prior year.

Ch. 9 HR, leave and attendance

Register staff, approve leave, check for weeks over 52 hours and close the month. Personal details stay outside the ledger.

Ch. 10 Payroll, entries, transfers and withholding

The company enters its rates and tax table and confirms the register. Download the transfer file and export the withholding figures. The app moves no money.

Ch. 11 Bills of materials and manufacturing orders

Shortages show first. On completion the inventory module moves the stock, and undoing it brings the stock back.

Ch. 12 Expenses

Attach a receipt and submit, then refuse, resubmit, approve and pay. A colleague with no part in it does not see the document.

Ch. 13 CRM and projects

A win creates a draft quote, and confirming a service order creates a project. Short of evidence, the forecast is given only as a range.
