Industry AI Solutions

Records that let you undo work also guide your next decision

Records kept to undo AI work help us learn from the past, simulate the future, and give people and agents the same evidence for decisions. All figures in the examples below come from virtual companies.

See the flowSee the Three ProductsContact us
Undo work→Learn from the past→Simulate the future→Share the evidence

1 · Undo work

When AI does the work, some of it will need undoing

Starting with 1,000 units in stock, an agent misreads demand and reserves 200 at 9 a.m. on Monday. An employee ships 30 at 11 a.m. on Tuesday, and approval is rejected at 2 p.m. that day. Undoing only the agent’s reservation must leave 970 units, with the employee’s shipment intact.

In our ERPAfter processing a tax invoice, say, “I’ll cancel what we just did.” The server checks the scope and executes the cancellation; the LLM does not control it. The ledger keeps 21 processing rows and 21 cancellation rows, erases none, and shows a net change of 0 for this task.

2 · Learn from the past

Records kept for undo also reveal insights

Records kept for undo also explain why sales were low last summer. Inventory history distinguishes weak demand from stockouts. Sales records alone suggest Q3 air circulator demand of 3,972 units, but accounting for 146 stockout days over the past year raises the estimate to 6,545.

Check past forecasts against actual resultsWe ran the same forecast using the ledger as it stood a year earlier and compared it with actual results. Revenue in all 3 completed quarters fell within the predicted ranges, and the top product was correct in 2 of those quarters.

3 · Simulate the future

Use the past to simulate the future before acting

With past records available, ask, “What happens if we increase that product’s order quantity to 1.5 times the current amount?” It estimates about KRW 46.64 million more revenue and KRW 7.72 million more inventory value over the next year. This question adds 0 new ledger records.

See the basis and limits of the calculationThe same models used on the ERP screen calculate the numbers; the LLM does not invent them. The predicted ranges for a year ahead are too narrow, so the system warns when looking beyond 180 days.

4 · Share the evidence

People and agents decide from the same evidence

People and agents can review the same simulation results. The numbers people see on the ERP screen and agents receive through the AI connection standard (MCP) come from the same ledger, models, and permissions.

What is implemented todayToday, a person asks and the agent answers. Having agents run simulations on their own before deciding and acting is the next step.

Test first, act, and undo mistakes.

It all comes from one ledger that keeps its records

State-based data keeps only the latest value. Event-based data (event sourcing) adds a row for each event and calculates current values from those records.

Approval was rejected at Tue 14:00. The table does not show why stock fell by 200. Restoring 1,000 would also erase the 30 units shipped by an employee.

We tested it

We connected uEngine ERP and Process GPT Lite through MCP and ran voucher processing, cancellation in chat, and question-and-answer conversations 5 times each with a virtual company locally; all passed. These are not production results, and the Lite integration will be released soon.

Upload a tax invoice, then cancel in chat (Korean UI and narration)

  • Upload, and it asksUpload a tax invoice file, and it reads the supplier and amount of KRW 1,100,000, then asks, “Shall I process this in ERP?”
  • Accept, and it processes the invoiceIt finds the supplier and product, posts the voucher, and makes the payment, while inventory, accounting, and tax modules use the records to create goods receipts, journal entries, and VAT totals.
  • Cancel in chatSay, “I'll cancel what we just did,” and the server checks the scope, cancels the payment, and posts a credit note in reverse order; the LLM does not control cancellation.
  • Check the numbersBoth the original work and cancellation remain, and this task’s net changes—bank deposits -KRW 1,100,000, input VAT +KRW 100,000, merchandise +KRW 1,000,000, and air circulator motors +20 units—are all 0 after cancellation, which does not mean the company’s total balances are zero.
  • Stop when cancellation is blockedIf goods have shipped, the accounting period is locked, or someone else has continued the work, it explains why and changes nothing.

Just ask in chat

  • What will sell the most in each quarter next year?In the virtual company, air circulators have a probability of over 99% of ranking first in Q3 2027; humidifiers have a 39% probability in Q4.
  • Can I trust this forecast?Using the ledger from a year earlier, revenue in all 3 completed quarters fell within the predicted ranges, and the top product was right in 2 quarters.
  • What happens if we increase that product's order quantity to 1.5 times the current amount?For the virtual company, it estimates about KRW 46.64 million more revenue and KRW 7.72 million more inventory value over the next year, while this conversation adds 0 new ledger records.

Where do the three products stand?

We compare features using the same four questions and add access details; all figures and screens come from virtual companies and synthetic data.

uEngine ERP

Event sourcing supports undo and testing before acting. The Process GPT Lite connection has been tested and will be released soon, but tax screens await expert review, and the app does not directly issue electronic tax invoices or submit electronic tax filings.

Product overviewTutorial

uEngine MES

A demonstration reference implementation of an event-sourced manufacturing execution system. Each action has a matching cancelling action, but there is no undo screen for people to use; source code is private, so contact us for a demo.

uEngine WMS

A Process GPT sample app that logs every write; it is not event sourced and has no undo feature. It provides 83 MCP tools and public source code.
