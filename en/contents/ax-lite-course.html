Starter · Process GPT Lite · Training with installation

AI Work Class for Your Company

Learn with your own company’s work, then use it right at your company. Bring one real task, learn how to handle it together with AI, and we carry out the initial installation of Process GPT Lite in the agreed environment with you. Afterwards, you can keep improving your work with a consultant through a mentoring subscription.

View the curriculum Transformation program Mentoring subscription Request a demo
Starter

A starting course for small businesses and individuals

Learn with one real task and start using Process GPT Lite right away. Larger organizations where several departments move together start differently, using the Agent Storming Methodology to settle work boundaries and a first pilot first.

Learn · Build · Grow

Learn it, apply it to your work, and improve it together

Where AX Transformation Consulting Training covers the transformation strategy for executives and development teams, this course is where front-line practitioners change their own work directly with Process GPT Lite. It doesn’t end with a lecture: the same system is set up inside your company, and you use it from the next day.

STEP 1 · Training

A hands-on course you learn step by step through scenarios

Practice everything yourself, from the first chat to writing documents, building work apps, team sharing and process automation. In each of the 12 modules you follow along with a demo video and finish an output you can use in your work.

STEP 2 · Deployment

Initial Process GPT Lite installation included in the training

We install the system used in the training on one agreed company server and connect it to the LLM your company uses. The skills and apps built during the exercises move over too, for use in real work. LLM and server costs and custom integrations are separate, and we explain them up front.

STEP 3 · Adoption

Monthly, always-on online support through the mentoring subscription

A consultant keeps supporting diagnosis, process innovation, and training and change management every month, pitched to employees, teams and executives. There are three plans, from KRW 2 million to KRW 10 million a month.

From individual work improvement to organizational change

Even when individuals get faster, the organization’s work stays the same if the ‘handoff’ between tasks is blocked. Following the stages of AX transformation, the course picks one bottleneck, lifts it into a skill and, once that is proven, weaves it into a process.

Stage 1 Assistant AI advises and people execute. Start with chat, context and creating documents. Modules 0–4
Stage 2 Digital colleague · Human in the Loop Instructions repeated three or more times become skills the whole team can call. People make the key judgments. Modules 5–8
Stage 3 Autonomous enterprise · Human on the Loop Skills are linked by events so the flow does the work itself. People see only approvals and exceptions. Modules 9–11

By the end of the course, participants change how they work: from ‘performer’ to ‘agent boss’, who sets goals and context, verifies the results and captures the way of working as skills.

Included in the training

Installation is part of the training — the same system you trained on, at your company

The training includes installing Process GPT Lite in the agreed company environment. Process GPT Lite is open source (github.com/uengine-oss/process-gpt-lite) and installs as a Docker Compose bundle.

What the training includes

  • Installation with Docker Compose on one server (or PC) your company provides — once
  • Initial setup: admin account, login email, and choice of configuration profile (lite or full)
  • Connection to the LLM your company uses: OpenAI-compatible APIs, in-house LLMs, vLLM and more
  • Moving the skills and panel apps built in the exercises over and checking that they work
  • One walkthrough of upgrades and backups (remote or on-site, by agreement)

Separate (explained up front in the consultation)

  • Server, cloud and network costs, LLM and API usage fees, and external service costs
  • Enterprise deployments with Kubernetes, redundancy and more (Process GPT)
  • Custom integration with in-house SSO, groupware and core business systems
  • Migration of existing data and long-term managed operation, travel, additional mentoring, maintenance

Installation requirements: Docker Engine 24+ (Compose v2), at least 6 GB of memory (8 GB recommended), at least 4 CPU cores and at least 20 GB of disk. If you’d like to try installing it yourself, the single line below is all it takes.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/uengine-oss/process-gpt-lite/main/install/get.sh | bash

AX Transformation Program

From upfront diagnosis to change management — how the AX transformation program flows

Training alone doesn’t change an organization. We first diagnose your company’s value stream, find the work that agents and automation can take over at the bottlenecks, redesign processes and roles, and then make the change stick with training for each audience and change management. The mentoring subscription carries this flow forward month after month.

Diagnosis

Upfront diagnosis — value stream analysis

What it does
Map the flow from a customer request coming in to value being delivered (the value stream). Separate the processing time and waiting time at each step.
Participants
Executives (goals and strategy), practitioners from each team involved in the flow, uEngine consultants
Method
Interviews with the people in charge and a review of handoff and work records. If Process GPT Lite is installed, process mining of the accumulated chat and work records confirms which flows actually repeat.
Output
Value stream map (processing time · waiting time · lead time per step)
Period
Weeks 1–2
▶ Turn Repeated Work into a Process (Process Mining)
2
Diagnosis

Finding bottlenecks — the blockage is in the handoffs between tasks

What it does
Find handoff and waiting points, rework, approval queues, and places where the same information is entered again in several places. Because the slowest segment sets overall throughput, speeding up anything that isn’t the bottleneck can wait.
Participants
Team leads, practitioners, consultants
Method
Marking waiting and rework on the value stream map, and prioritizing from a Theory of Constraints (ToC) perspective
Tools
The Process GPT Lite “Improve” screen — see bottlenecks, value stream and constraints, and the critical path with improvements calculated in advance straight from execution records
Output
A prioritized list of bottlenecks
Period
Weeks 2–3

Participants
Practitioners, team leads, consultants
Method
Test right away in Process GPT Lite with real data: conversation context, IeumOffice documents, Craft apps, drafts from a single email
Output
A list of candidate agent applications with expected benefits (time and quality), and test results

Process innovation

Changing the process itself — at the organizational level

What it does
Rather than stopping at bolting AI onto existing flows, redesign the To-Be process. Cut handoff steps, form cross-functional teams in which product, marketing and sales move together, and let product and marketing run in parallel from one shared spec. Roles that did the work directly (performers) become agent bosses.
Participants
Executives (adjusting decision rights), team leads, consultants
Method
To-Be process design, redefined roles and responsibilities (RACI), and the new flow reflected in shared-folder permissions and owner notifications
Output
To-Be process and RACI
Period
Weeks 5–6

Where AX Leads

An organization that turns customer response into action fast

The end point of AX isn’t ‘a company that uses AI’, but a company that starts from the value it delivers to customers, builds product and market experiments together, and feeds the market’s response straight into the next round of execution.

AGILE

Agile rhythm

Build and release in short cycles, measure the response and improve. Agents support research and production, shortening the time to the next experiment.

MARKETING FIRST

Marketing-first development

Start from “whose problem do we solve, and with what value?” A single concept carries through to the product build and to preparing brochures, landing pages and demo videos.

SPEC-DRIVEN

Parallel execution from one spec

A shared spec covering target customers, usage scenarios, key features, acceptance criteria, messaging and metrics drives product and marketing together. Teams spend less time re-explaining and waiting on each other.

GROWTH HACKING

Validating several hypotheses in the market

Instead of a single A/B test, put out several hypotheses at once (value proposition, messaging, usage flow, delivery model), measure them by conversion, activation, return visits and customer feedback, and feed the results into the next spec.
