Why Diagnosis First

The slowest step sets the pace for the whole company

This is the starting point of the theory of constraints. Bring AI into a step that is not the bottleneck, and individuals get faster while the speed at which value reaches customers stays the same. That is why this program puts diagnosis before training, starts with a single bottleneck, and expands as it measures.

CONSTRAINT

Find the bottleneck first

We map the value stream and separate processing time from waiting time at each step. The time you can cut usually sits in handoffs and approval waits between pieces of work.

PEOPLE · AI · RULES

Choose a fix that fits the bottleneck

We divide the work in the bottleneck among agents, automation, rules and people. Where needed, we redesign the process and the roles themselves.

CHANGE

Change sticks only when people change

Training tailored to executives, team leads, practitioners and AX champions, plus monthly mentoring, turns the new way of working into a habit.

MEASURE

Pilot → measure → expand

We pilot on a single bottleneck and measure lead time, throughput and rework before and after. Once the effect is confirmed, we move on to the next bottleneck and to other teams.

7 Steps

From diagnosis to change management — the 7 steps

For each step: the activities, who takes part, the methods, the deliverables and the timing. A mentoring subscription carries this flow forward month by month.

  1. Diagnosis
  2. Bottlenecks
  3. Agents
  4. Automation
  5. Process innovation
  6. Training
  7. Change management
1
Diagnosis

Upfront diagnosis — value stream analysis

What it does
Map the flow from a customer request coming in to value being delivered (the value stream). Separate the processing time and waiting time at each step.
Participants
Executives (goals and strategy), practitioners from each team involved in the flow, uEngine consultants
Method
Lay out events and boundaries on a real work case with simplified EventStorming. Interviews with the people involved and a review of handoff and work records. If Process GPT Lite is installed, process mining of accumulated conversation and work logs shows which flows really repeat.
Output
Value stream map (processing time · waiting time · lead time per step)
Period
Weeks 1–2
▶ Turn Repeated Work into a Process (Process Mining)
2
Diagnosis

Finding bottlenecks — the blockage is in the handoffs between tasks

What it does
Find handoff and waiting points, rework, approval queues, and places where the same information is entered again in several places. Because the slowest segment sets overall throughput, speeding up anything that isn’t the bottleneck can wait.
Participants
Team leads, practitioners, consultants
Method
Marking waiting and rework on the value stream map, and prioritizing from a Theory of Constraints (ToC) perspective
Tools
The Process GPT Lite “Improve” screen — see bottlenecks, value stream and constraints, and the critical path with improvements calculated in advance straight from execution records
Output
A prioritized list of bottlenecks
Period
Weeks 2–3
3
Agents

Start with what agents can solve

What it does
Among the work inside the bottleneck, pick what an AI agent can do or help with: drafting, research, review and checking, and document work that spans several tools. People become the ‘agent boss’ who sets goals and completion criteria and approves the results (Human in the Loop).
Participants
Practitioners, team leads, consultants
Method
Test right away in Process GPT Lite with real data: conversation context, IeumOffice documents, Craft apps, drafts from a single email
Output
A list of candidate agent applications with expected benefits (time and quality), and test results
Period
Weeks 3–4
▶ From One Email to Floor Plan, Proposal and Quote ▶ Agent Workroom
4
Automation

Finding what can be automated

What it does
Pick work that repeats three or more times and follows a set procedure, and automate it. Repeated instructions become skills, work at set times becomes scheduled missions, and multi-step work becomes registered processes.
Participants
Practitioners, the team’s AX champions, consultants
Method
Skills · scheduled missions · building processes in chat (saved as Process GPT BPMN processes) · drafting triggered by an incoming email
Output
Automation backlog (target · method · owner · priority)
Period
Weeks 4–5
▶ Connections, Skills and Scheduled Missions ▶ Build a Process in Chat
5
Process innovation

Changing the process itself — at the organizational level

What it does
Rather than stopping at bolting AI onto existing flows, redesign the To-Be process. Cut handoff steps, form cross-functional teams in which product, marketing and sales move together, and let product and marketing run in parallel from one shared spec. Roles that did the work directly (performers) become agent bosses.
Participants
Executives (adjusting decision rights), team leads, consultants
Method
To-Be process design, redefined roles and responsibilities (RACI), and the new flow reflected in shared-folder permissions and owner notifications
Output
To-Be process and RACI
Period
Weeks 5–6
6
Training

Change management training — training by role, applied on the job

What it does
Plan training by audience for the people who will actually run the new process.
Participants
Executives, team leads, practitioners, AX champions
Method
  • Executives — AX vision and KPIs, delegating decision rights (AX Transformation Consulting Training, Track A)
  • Team leads — running an agent-boss team, running experiment cycles and retrospectives
  • Practitioners — the 12-module practitioner course
  • AX champions — advanced course (skill and process design) and how to coach inside the company
Output
Training plan by audience (audience · content · schedule · completion criteria)
Period
Weeks 6–8
7
Change management

Company-wide change management program — pilot → measure → expand

What it does
Pilot with one bottleneck, measure before and after, and once the effect is confirmed, expand to the next bottleneck and to other teams. The cycle continues through monthly mentoring sessions, AX champions in each department, internal sharing (results and skills), and quarterly KPI reviews.
Participants
Company-wide (executives · team leads · AX champions · practitioners)
Method
Mentoring subscription, skill sharing with review and merge, reviews of lead time, throughput and rework metrics
Output
Quarterly KPI review and next quarter’s priorities
Period
Repeats every quarter from month 3
