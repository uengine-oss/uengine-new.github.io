uEngine Solutions Unveils ‘DreamVibe’, an AI-Powered Full-Lifecycle Development Automation Platform

Next-generation integrated platform launched to automate the entire development lifecycle with AI
uEngine Solutions has unveiled ‘DreamVibe’, a next-generation platform in which artificial intelligence (AI) automates the entire software development lifecycle, from business analysis to system operations.
DreamVibe goes beyond the limits of existing AI coding tools, which focused only on the development stage, by supporting the full Biz-Dev-Ops spectrum in an integrated way. In particular, its AI automation of business analysis based on Domain-Driven Design (DDD) and EventStorming is a differentiator that global competitors have yet to deliver.
